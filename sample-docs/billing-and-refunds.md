# Heyo Cloud — Billing, Subscriptions & Refund Policy

## 1. Subscription Plans & Pricing

Heyo offers three straightforward subscription tiers designed for teams of all sizes:

### Starter Plan
- **Price:** $29 / month (or $290 / year with 2 months free)
- **Included Seats:** Up to 3 operators
- **Conversations:** Up to 1,000 active visitor conversations per month
- **AI Agent Features:** Basic AI answering with standard intent classification
- **Storage:** 100MB knowledge base document storage
- **Support:** Community and email support (24-48h response time)

### Pro Plan
- **Price:** $79 / month (or $790 / year with 2 months free)
- **Included Seats:** Up to 10 operators ($15/month per additional operator seat)
- **Conversations:** Up to 5,000 active visitor conversations per month
- **AI Agent Features:** Autonomous RAG AI agent with vector grounding and dual-guardrail human handoff
- **Storage:** 1GB knowledge base document storage (PDF, Markdown, TXT)
- **Real-Time Transport:** Multi-tab synchronization across PartyKit Edge WebSockets
- **Support:** Priority email and live chat support (under 2h response time)

### Enterprise Plan
- **Price:** $299 / month (custom annual invoicing available)
- **Included Seats:** Unlimited operator seats
- **Conversations:** Unlimited conversations
- **AI Agent Features:** Custom fine-tuned classifier, dedicated HNSW vector partitions
- **Storage:** 20GB knowledge base storage with custom retention
- **SLA:** 99.99% uptime guarantee with dedicated customer success manager

## 2. Refund & Cancellation Policy

### 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
If you are not completely satisfied with Heyo Cloud within your first **14 days** of initial subscription, you are entitled to a full 100% refund—no questions asked. 
To request a refund, simply message support in the widget or email `billing@heyo.so` with your workspace ID.
- **Refund Processing Timeline:** Once requested and approved, refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days (standard bank settlement may take 5–10 days).

### Prorated Refunds for Annual Plans
Annual subscriptions cancelled after the 14-day window are eligible for a prorated refund for any unused full months remaining on the contract, minus a 10% administrative processing fee. Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable after the first 14 days, but remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

### How to Cancel Your Subscription
1. Navigate to **Dashboard Settings** > **Billing**.
2. Click **Manage Subscription** to access the Stripe billing customer portal.
3. Select **Cancel Plan**. Your account will retain all features until the end of the prepaid period.

## 3. Invoices & Payment Methods

- **Accepted Payments:** All major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and SEPA Direct Debit.
- **Invoice Receipts:** Invoices are automatically sent via email upon payment. You can also download PDF invoices anytime under **Dashboard** > **Billing** > **Invoice History**.
- **Sales Tax & VAT:** EU and UK customers can enter their VAT ID during checkout for reverse-charge tax exemption.
